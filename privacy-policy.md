# Privacy Policy - IntentRouter

IntentRouter is a browser extension that helps users compare SaaS and AI tools before purchase.

## Data collection

The extension does not collect, sell, or share personal browsing history.

IntentRouter analyzes the current webpage locally in the browser to detect whether the page may be related to pricing, checkout, software comparison, subscription, or product discovery.

## Affiliate disclosure

IntentRouter may display affiliate or referral links when a relevant offer, trial, comparison, discount, or alternative is available.

Affiliate links are only displayed after user interaction and only when they are related to the current user intent.

## No automated behavior

IntentRouter does not generate automatic clicks, does not inject hidden redirects, and does not perform cookie stuffing.
